AI risk sentiment and equity markets

Investigating the relationship between AI-related discourse and market movements.

In this article

Research questionApproach

Research question

Can shifts in AI-related sentiment provide information about subsequent equity-market returns?

This project compares news and social-media sentiment with movements in QQQ to explore whether these sources convey different signals.

Approach

Transformer-based sentiment analysis, aggregation of news and social-media observations, and Granger causality tests form the basis of the study.

Detailed methods, results and figures will be added from the final project materials.
