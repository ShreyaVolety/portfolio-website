Sentiment in SEC filings

Extracting financial risk signals from corporate disclosures.

In this article

Research questionApproach

Research question

What can the language of company filings tell us about potential financial risks?

This project examines corporate filings for holdings across ARK ETFs, using financial language models and domain-specific lexicons.

Approach

FinBERT and financial lexicons provide complementary ways to quantify sentiment and references to economic risks, including interest rates and inflation.

Detailed methods, results and figures will be added from the final project materials.
