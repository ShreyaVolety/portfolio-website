Overview

A collection of numerical methods for pricing derivative securities and estimating risk measures.

The implementations explore binomial pricing, Monte Carlo methods for path-dependent options and finite-difference Greeks.

Methods

Control variates, backward induction and numerical approximation provide different approaches to accuracy and computational efficiency.

Detailed implementations, examples and validation results will be added from the project materials.
